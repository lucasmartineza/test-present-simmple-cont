🕰️ Pasado simple: reglas rápidas

Regulares: verbo + -ed → play → played
Terminan en -e: solo + -d → like → liked
Consonante + y: la y cambia a -ied → try → tried (con vocal + y no cambia: play → played)
Verbos cortos (consonante-vocal-consonante): se duplica la última letra → stop → stopped
Negativo: didn't + verbo en forma base → She didn't like it (sin -ed)
Pregunta: (palabra de pregunta) + did + sujeto + verbo en forma base → What did you drink?
